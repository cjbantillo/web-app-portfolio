## Care Guardian - AI-Powered Eldercare Monitoring System

### Project Overview

Care Guardian is an innovative AI-powered eldercare monitoring startup designed specifically for the Philippine healthcare context. The solution combines IoT technology, artificial intelligence, and mobile applications to provide comprehensive senior care monitoring.

### Leadership Role

**Position:** CEO & Co-Founder

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Strategic planning and startup ideation
- Business model development and validation
- Comprehensive market research (TAM/SAM/SOM analysis)
- Pitch deck creation and competitive pitching
- Revenue projection and pricing strategy
- Government and hospital partnership development
- Product positioning and go-to-market strategy

### Technical Architecture

#### Core Features

- Real-time monitoring of seniors through AI-powered cameras
- Automated fall and inactivity detection
- Instant emergency alert notifications
- Non-intrusive caregiving solutions
- Community-based response coordination
- LGU and hospital system integration

#### Technology Stack

- **Hardware:** AI-powered camera hub (IoT-based)
- **Mobile Applications:** Android and iOS native apps
- **Backend Infrastructure:** Cloud-based (AWS/GCP architecture)
- **Communication:** SMS and in-app emergency alerts
- **Compliance:** Data privacy-compliant architecture
- **Integration:** LGU and hospital response systems

### Target Market

#### Primary Stakeholders

- Families with elderly members requiring care monitoring
- Hospitals and nursing homes seeking automation
- Local Government Units (LGUs) and barangay health workers
- Senior care facilities and retirement communities

### Technology Readiness Level (TRL)

**Current Status:** TRL 3 - Experimental Proof of Concept

**Development Roadmap:**

- **TRL 5 (2025-2026):** Pilot deployment with LGUs and hospitals
- **TRL 6-7 (2026):** Field validation and refinement
- **TRL 8 (2026-2027):** Commercial readiness
- **TRL 9 (2027):** Nationwide deployment and scaling
